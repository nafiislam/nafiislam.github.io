About

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at United International University. I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) with a CGPA of 3.97/4.00.

My research applies deep learning and protein and RNA language models to biological sequence problems, including post-translational modification sites, protein-carbohydrate binding, enzyme properties and RNA modifications. I have seven published papers in this area and am applying to PhD programs to continue this work in computational biology and machine learning.

Publications

Published

Predicting Protein-Carbohydrate Binding Sites: A Deep Learning Approach Integrating Protein Language Model Embeddings and Structural FeaturesUndergraduate thesis

Briefings in Bioinformatics

A deep-learning architecture that combines protein language model embeddings with structural features to predict protein-carbohydrate binding sites. Paper

Predicting C- and S-linked Glycosylation Sites from Protein Sequences Using Protein Language Models

Computers in Biology and Medicine

A hybrid deep-learning model that uses protein language model embeddings and contextual information to predict C- and S-linked glycosylation sites. Paper

StackGlyEmbed: Prediction of N-linked Glycosylation Sites Using Protein Language Models

Bioinformatics Advances

A stacking model that predicts N-linked glycosylation sites from protein language model embeddings using window-level and per-residue features. Paper

ResLysEmbed: A ResNet-Based Framework for Succinylated Lysine Residue Prediction Using Sequence and Language Model Embeddings

Bioinformatics Advances

A hybrid ResNet-based architecture that uses protein language model embeddings to identify succinylated lysine residues. Paper

NFEmbed: Modeling Nitrogenase Activity via Classification and Regression with Pretrained Protein Embeddings

Bioinformatics Advances

Stacking ensemble models that predict microbial strains with high nitrogenase potential and outperform state-of-the-art methods. Paper

Predicting RNA 5-Hydroxymethylcytosine Modification with Deep Learning Models Using RNA Language Model Embeddings

PLOS ONE

A dual-branch deep-learning model that predicts RNA 5-hydroxymethylcytosine modifications from RNA language model embeddings, with biological interpretation of the learned features. Paper

DeepBCTPred: Deep Learning-Based Prediction of Bladder Cancer Tissues from Endoscopic Images

Bioinformatics Advances

An image-generation pipeline with a genetic algorithm for image selection, combining handcrafted features with features learned by convolutional neural networks. Paper

Preprint

Prediction of Protein-Carbohydrate Binding Sites from Protein Primary Sequence

bioRxiv

StackCBEmbed, an ensemble model that classifies protein-carbohydrate binding at the residue level by combining sequence-based features with protein language model embeddings. Preprint

A multi-task framework that predicts enzyme parameters (Topt, Tm, pHopt) with better accuracy and interpretability than state-of-the-art methods.
